Fig 8a · The scorecard, part 1: connectivity extensions

Extending the connection: 4 won, 1 mixed, 1 flopped

Six products that sell or deepen the connection itself: handsets, home broadband and in-plan TV. All six are Bundle plays.

The pattern: Bundle wins when the alternative is nothing (a 2G phone, no home broadband). The one flop, JioPhone Next, was a smartphone. It needed buyers to prefer it, and Xiaomi already had that preference. These wins extend telecom. They don't leave it.

As of 23 Oct 2026. Mode = the dominant strategic investment mode (author's assessment).
