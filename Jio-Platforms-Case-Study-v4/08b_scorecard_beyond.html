Fig 8b · The scorecard, part 2: beyond connectivity

Leaving the connection: 1 won, 2 mixed, 3 flopped

Six products that compete for attention outside the SIM: streaming, music, commerce, video calls, messaging and browsing.

The pattern: beyond connectivity, Jio's clearest win, JioHotstar, came from combining its distribution with a scarce asset it bought (IPL rights, Disney's catalogue). Buying a non-scarce asset (music) produced a mixed result. None of the four standalone Build attempts has displaced an established specialist.

As of 23 Oct 2026. JioCinema existed as a bundled Jio app from 2016; it only became a contender after the 2023 IPL rights (Buy), so it is scored as part of JioHotstar. Scores are the author's judgement, made with hindsight. Outcomes judge adoption and competitive position against the "win if" criterion; standalone financial returns are not public for most products.
