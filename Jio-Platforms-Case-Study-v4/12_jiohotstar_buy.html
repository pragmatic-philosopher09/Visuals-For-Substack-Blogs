Why this Buy worked (author's read): distribution plus an asset that was scarce. Live IPL can only be watched in one place, so anyone who wants it has to come to JioHotstar. Compare JioSaavn: every app licenses the same songs, so buying Saavn bought scale but not exclusivity. (Execution, pricing and discovery mattered in both cases too.)
