Fig 14b · The economics underneath (conceptual)

Each mode has its own cash shape

A conceptual model, not Jio data. It shows the typical shape of cumulative cash for each mode and how each can fail to reach the upswing.

Bundle: shallow dip, fast return

Costs are device subsidies and network capex Jio was spending anyway. The return shows up as ARPU and lower churn, not a new revenue line.

Buy: deep dip, then it repeats

Rights are paid upfront and expire. The IPL contract ends in 2027, so the dip comes back at every renewal.

Build: slow bleed, uncertain upswing

Engineering and acquisition costs keep accruing. It only turns if users form a habit. For JioChat, JioMeet and JioPages, they didn't.

Illustrative shapes only: no scale, no payback period is calculated or implied. Not drawn from Jio's accounts.
