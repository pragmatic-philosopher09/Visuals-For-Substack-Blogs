The prediction (author's view): Jio has a more direct route to AI value through rungs 1–3: embedding AI, renting Gemini and selling compute. Rung 4 isn't destined to fail, but it would have to beat Jio's own consumer Build record. One opening worth watching: Indic-language, voice-first AI for users the global labs don't serve well. That is the same "the alternative is nothing" condition that made JioPhone work.
