A heavy AI user could cost more than they pay Jio

Data costs fall with scale. AI inference costs grow with usage. That's why "bundle it free" doesn't carry over to AI automatically.

Retail price of Google AI Pro, about 9× Jio's ARPU. That is a retail price, not Jio's cost; terms are undisclosed. Who bears the inference cost isn't public.
The strategic consequence: Jio should bundle AI only where a partner pays for inference, or where it controls the cost (its own compute running small Indic models). If Jio had to bear the full cost of heavy frontier-model usage, ~₹130 of EBITDA per connection would not go far. Whether the offer pays depends on the partnership terms, actual usage, and any retention or acquisition benefit.

Q1 FY27: ARPU ₹215.6; EBITDA per connection = ₹20,865 cr ÷ 533.3M ÷ 3 months ≈ ₹130 (EBITDA covers all of Jio's businesses, not just mobile). ARPU, EBITDA and AI cost are different measures, shown together only for scale. AI inference costs are the author's illustrative assumptions, not Jio data: light ≈ a few short chats a week on a small model; medium ≈ daily use on a mid-tier model; heavy ≈ frequent long-context use of a frontier model, images and voice. Real costs vary widely by model and are falling, but usage is rising.
