What would prove this thesis wrong

A thesis worth holding should say what would break it. Here are four signals and the metrics to watch.

Signal 1 · Build wins
A Jio-built app earns daily habit on its own

JioMart quick commerce passing Blinkit in orders, or a Jio AI app keeping strong DAU/MAU without a bundle propping it up.

Watch: JioMart daily orders and market share · Jio AI app DAU/MAU
Signal 2 · Pricing power fades
Pricing power fades

ARPU growth has already slowed to 3.3% YoY. If it keeps slowing between hikes, or the next hike costs enough users to shrink mobile revenue, the pricing power from consolidation is running out.

Watch: ARPU growth YoY (+3.3% today, vs Airtel +5.6%) · subscribers lost per 1% of ARPU gained · monthly churn (1.6% today)
Signal 3 · Buy gets too expensive
The IPL renewal (2027) wipes out JioHotstar's margin

If renewal costs grow faster than paying subscribers, Buy becomes rent Jio can't afford.

Watch: IPL 2028–32 media rights price · JioStar paid subs and ARPU · digital services revenue growth (+20% today)
Signal 4 · The platform premium shows up
Digital services become a large, separately valued share of revenue

If non-connectivity revenue grows from roughly 12% (FY26 non-RJIL share, a rough proxy) to a third of Jio's revenue with healthy margins, the platform pitch is supported by numbers.

Watch: Digital services share of revenue · the market multiple vs the ~12.3× IPO starting point
