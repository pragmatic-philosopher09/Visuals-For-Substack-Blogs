Stop 01 · 2016

Price mattered. The slot mattered more.

Most Indian phones were dual-SIM. So nobody had to give up their number. They just filled the empty slot with a free Jio SIM. About 100 million users in roughly 170 days.

Stop 02 · 2016–2019

About a dozen operators went in. Three came out.

With three players, matching a rival's tariff hike is the rational move. In July 2024 Jio raised prices 12–27%, lost ~10.9M connections, and still made ~5% more.

Rule 2 · pricing power came from market structure

Stop 03 · Every 28 days

The busiest station isn't an app. It's the recharge screen.

About 90% of Indian connections are prepaid. Plans expire every 28, 56 or 84 days, so users keep coming back to pay.

It isn't sold to anyone. It's where everything else is sold.

Build line · make your own app

Build a standalone app, and the trains stop coming.

Distribution gets an app installed. Only preference gets it opened again tomorrow.

Change here for WhatsAppIn 2020 Jio sold ~10% of itself to Meta, and JioMart launched inside WhatsApp, the messenger JioChat was built to rival. It stopped building habit and started renting it.

Buy line · rent what's scarce

Buy something scarce, and the platforms fill up.

Music isn't scarce: every app licenses the same songs. Live IPL is in one place. Jio didn't build a reason to subscribe. It bought one.
