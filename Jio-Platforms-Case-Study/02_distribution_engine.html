Fig 2 · Act I: Building the distribution

Make data almost free, then fill the empty SIM slot

Jio didn't ask anyone to switch. It asked for the second SIM slot most Indian phones already had.

Company-reported, period end.

The dual-SIM wedge

Users kept their old SIM for calls and added Jio for free data. Switching cost: zero. Jio took the second slot without asking anyone to give up their first SIM.

Speed

~100 million users in about 170 days after the Sept 2016 launch, on free "Welcome" and "Happy New Year" offers that ran until March 2017.

What it bought

A billing relationship with 533M connections (not unique people, given dual-SIM), 285M of them on 5G. Prepaid connections come back to recharge every 28–84 days.

Sources: TRAI; Reliance/Jio quarterly reports (Q2 FY26: ARPU ₹211.4, 38.7 GB/user; Q1 FY27: ARPU ₹215.6, 43.7 GB/user, 533.3M subs). Grey bars: TRAI average cost per GB as cited in its Aug 2019 release (₹269 in 2014 → ₹11.78 in 2018). Blue bars use a different measure (Jio's realised revenue per GB) and are not strictly comparable with TRAI's series.
