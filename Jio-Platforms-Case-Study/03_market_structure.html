Fig 3 · Act I: The market Jio left behind

A dozen operators became three

The free-data price war didn't just win customers. It changed the structure of the market, and that is where Jio's later pricing power came from.

Wireless subscriber share (%), March of each year

Who left the market

  1. 2017Telenor India sold to Airtel. Tata Teleservices' mobile business followed (2019).
  2. 2017Reliance Communications (Anil Ambani) shut 2G/voice services.
  3. 2018Aircel filed for bankruptcy.
  4. 2018Vodafone and Idea merged to survive, becoming Vi.

Then prices moved together

RoundJio hikePattern
Dec 2019up to ~40%All three within days
Dec 2021~20%Airtel first, Vi & Jio followed
Jul 202412–27%Jio first, Airtel & Vi next day

Matching price moves reflect market structure. They are not, on their own, evidence of coordination.

Source: TRAI monthly subscription reports, rounded and approximate. "Others" includes BSNL/MTNL and operators that later exited. 2026 is July 2026. TRAI's revised reporting includes M2M connections, which lifts Airtel's share.
