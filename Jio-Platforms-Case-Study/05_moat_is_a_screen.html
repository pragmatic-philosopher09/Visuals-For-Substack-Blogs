The visit is scheduled. Prepaid plans expire, so users come back on the plan's schedule, not only when they happen to want something. Not every recharge happens in MyJio; many go through UPI apps and retailers.

The best slots go to bought products. JioHotstar (IPL rights, Disney merger) and Gemini (Google) take the hero banner and the "Most chosen" plan.
