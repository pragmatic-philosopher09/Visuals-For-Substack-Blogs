Fig 7 · The scorecard

Twelve consumer bets beyond the core SIM, on one map

Jio's wins cluster where the product reuses the existing relationship and needs little preference. Where preference was needed, Jio won only when it bought it.

Bundle Buy Build | ✓ Won ◐ Mixed ✕ Flopped
x · Reuse of the Jio relationship

0–3 separate app, separate decision. 4–6 uses Jio login, billing or app grid, but users still opt in. 7–10 arrives inside the plan, SIM, handset or router.

y · Preference needed

0–3 utility with no strong incumbent. 4–6 alternatives exist but users aren't attached. 7–10 competes with a product users actively choose (habit, taste or network effects).

Outcome (as of Oct 2026)

Won: reached its stated ambition at scale and is still core. Mixed: survived at scale but trails the leader or missed its ambition. Flopped: shut down, marginal usage, or publicly missed targets.
