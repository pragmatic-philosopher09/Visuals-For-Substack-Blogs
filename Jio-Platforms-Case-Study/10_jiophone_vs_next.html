The buyer compared it to

Their ₹1,000 2G Nokia, with costly voice and no data

The offer

"Effectively free" with a ~₹1,500 refundable deposit, tied to a cheap Jio plan

Why it worked

It wasn't competing on specs. It was a plan with a screen attached, and nothing on the market matched it.

The buyer compared it to

A Redmi or Realme at a similar price, with a better camera, more RAM and a brand they liked

The offer

₹6,499, or ₹1,999 down plus EMI, launched in the 2021 chip shortage

Why it missed

Once it was a smartphone, buyers judged it as a smartphone. The bundle couldn't create preference in a market with strong brands. Reported sales were far below the early ambitions.

The lesson: a bundle works when the alternative is nothing. It struggles when the alternative is something users already like.
