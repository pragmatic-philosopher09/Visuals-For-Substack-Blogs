The same year, a bigger move

Three months before JioMeet launched, Jio took ₹43,574 cr from Meta for ~10% of Jio Platforms. JioMart then launched inside WhatsApp, the messenger JioChat was built to rival.

In messaging, Jio stopped trying to win daily habit and started renting access to it. JioMeet looks, in hindsight, like the last attempt to Build in that space.
