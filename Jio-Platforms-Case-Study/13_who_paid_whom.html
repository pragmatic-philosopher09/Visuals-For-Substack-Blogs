Fig 13 · Follow the money

Jio sells distribution and buys preference

The money tells you what each side thinks Jio has, and what it lacks.

What the world pays Jio for: access to 500M+ Indian connections and the screen many of them recharge on. Meta wanted WhatsApp commerce. Google wanted Android and AI distribution.
What Jio pays the world for: things people choose on their own, like live cricket, Disney's catalogue and frontier AI models. Jio could not build any of these, so it rents or acquires them.

Figures: Meta ₹43,574 cr (Apr 2020); Google ₹33,737 cr (Jul 2020); IPL digital rights ₹23,758 cr (2023–27); Reliance growth capital into JioStar ~₹11,500 cr; Google AI Pro offer ~₹35,100 retail value per eligible user (18 months), funded and shared between Google and Jio on undisclosed terms. Not all flows are comparable (equity vs. rights vs. retail value); arrows show direction, widths are indicative.
