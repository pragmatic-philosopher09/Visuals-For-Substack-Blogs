Proposed North Star / Platform scope

Weekly returning players

Distinct players with gameplay in both the current and previous calendar week.

L1 / Discovery effectiveness

Was the match useful?

L2 · Impression-to-play
Exposed users who play ÷ exposed users, by surface and fixed attribution window.
L2 · Early exit
Early first-session exits ÷ first launches; predefine the threshold.

L1 / Successful activation

Did play actually begin?

L2 · Launch success
Successful launches ÷ attempts.
L2 · Time to first action
Median and p90 from entry to a meaningful action; report non-completion separately.

L1 / Repeat participation

Was there another visit?

L2 · Platform D7
Day-0 new players returning anywhere on day 7 ÷ day-0 new players.
L2 · Repeat co-play
Initial co-players co-playing again within 7 days ÷ eligible initial co-players.
GuardrailsSatisfactionCrashesUnwanted contactPurchase regretCreator exposure

Diagnostic, not additive. Use fixed calendar weeks/timezone and cohorts. Return is a proxy, not enjoyment. Creator retention analytics are experience-level, unlike our platform metrics.
