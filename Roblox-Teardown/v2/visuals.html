The detail behind the pictures

Metric definitions & scope

All metrics here are author-proposed. Weekly returning players are distinct players with gameplay in both the current and previous calendar week, using one fixed reporting timezone. The L1/L2 map is diagnostic, not an arithmetic decomposition. Return behavior is not proof of enjoyment.

Impression-to-play
Exposed users who play ÷ exposed users. Fix the surface and attribution window.
Early exit
Early first-session exits ÷ first launches. Predefine the threshold.
Launch success
Successful launches ÷ launch attempts.
First meaningful action
Median and p90 time from entry to a creator-defined meaningful action. Report non-completion separately.
Platform D7 retention
Day-0 new players who play anywhere on the platform on day 7 ÷ day-0 new players.
Repeat co-play
Initial co-players who co-play again within 7 days ÷ eligible initial co-players, in a fixed cohort.

Roblox's published creator retention metrics are experience-level, unlike our platform-level North Star and D7. No baseline or target is claimed. More safety reports can reflect more harm or better reporting; interpret them with other evidence.

“Before we join” — full test design

Audit the target device/account for equivalent existing UI first. Validate unmet need with observed group decisions and verify available metadata, creator-guidance accuracy, and eligibility restrictions.

For eligible adult groups, assign a stable group at its first eligible details view before exposure. Compare current details with the same details plus the summary. Define group identity and avoid cross-variant member exposure.

Primary outcome: groups reaching a shared server within five minutes of that first view ÷ all randomized groups. Count non-joins and failures; one observation per group. Pair with sampled “matched expectations” feedback and check nonresponse bias.

Set sample size, minimum meaningful effect, duration, and guardrail limits using baseline data before testing. Monitor crashes, unwanted contact, and purchase regret. Preserve all age, privacy, and parental restrictions. Five minutes is a proposed window, not a benchmark. No experiment has been run.
