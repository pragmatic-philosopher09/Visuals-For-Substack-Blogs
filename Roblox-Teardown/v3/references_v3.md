## 1. Primary documents
- [Roblox Q3 2026 shareholder letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/content_files/Q3-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf), 29 October 2026
  - p. 3: revenue $1.6B (+16%), bookings $1.7B (above the high end of guidance), 138M average DAUs (+12% QoQ), ~34B hours engaged (+17% QoQ), operating cash flow $395M, free cash flow $364M
  - p. 4: "people come to Roblox to play with their friends"; ~20% of the US under-18 gaming market; ~30% of US 13–17s use Roblox daily
  - pp. 5–6: age-checked DAU split of 34% under 13, 37% aged 13–17, 29% adults (37% in the US), up from 27%/33% in Q2; US adults monetize ~50% above under-18s; 64% age-check penetration overall, over 80% among 13–17s
  - p. 6: users chatting or on voice +24% QoQ; 1.6B chat messages a day; Friends chat tab, Quick Words, Roblox Plus
  - p. 7: "Recommended for You" changes "drove meaningful improvements in overall platform retention and monetization"; ~40% of the weekly top 100 recommended games were created in the last three months
  - p. 11: DAUs −9% YoY, hours −15% YoY, bookings per hour +4%, 30M monthly unique payers (−16%), bookings per payer +5%
  - p. 12: DevEx $403M (24% of bookings); infrastructure and trust & safety $258M (15%); net loss $230M; adjusted EBITDA $138M
  - p. 14: Q4 bookings guidance $1,890–2,000M
  - p. 19: age-check methodology note (figures are estimates and cover only age-checked users)
  - p. 22: reconciliation, revenue $1,572M and bookings $1,697M
- [Roblox Q2 2026 shareholder letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/Roblox-Q2-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf), 30 July 2026
  - p. 2: recommendation changes favouring retentive games "at the expense of near term monetization"
  - pp. 3–4: DevEx rate increases
  - pp. 5–6: recommendation signals, 28-day window, play-through and first-play bounce
  - p. 6 (footnote 4): first-play bounce definition; Kids and Select accounts
  - p. 7: off-platform co-play communication "may not align with our safety standards"
  - p. 9: top-10 games ≈20% of hours, down from ~30%

## 3. Game-page audit (1 November 2026)
Checked through Roblox's public game-details and age-recommendation endpoints (`games.roblox.com/v1/games`, `apis.roblox.com/experience-guidelines-api`).

| Game | Server limit | Private servers |
|---|---|---|
| DOORS (universe 2440500124; maturity Mild: fear, violence) | 50 | Not offered |
| The Mimic | 45 | Not offered |
| Pressure | 50 | Not offered |
| Break In (Story) | 45 | Not offered |

Pages show genre, maturity label, server limit, live players, server list and store. Group size, round length, whether you can learn as you go, and how a party starts together are absent or only in the creator's free-text description (DOORS's description mentions "entering without a guide"). Server limit is not a group size.
