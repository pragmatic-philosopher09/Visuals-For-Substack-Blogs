The detail behind the pictures

Metric definitions & scope

All metrics here are author-proposed. Weekly returning players are distinct players with gameplay in both the current and previous calendar week, in one fixed reporting timezone. The L1/L2 map is diagnostic, not an arithmetic decomposition. Return behavior is not proof of enjoyment.

Groups that get in together
Randomized eligible groups with all members in one server within five minutes of the first game-page view ÷ all randomized groups. Count non-joins.
Impression-to-play
Exposed users who play ÷ exposed users. Fix the surface and attribution window.
First-play bounce
First-time launches that exit within a fixed short threshold without meaningful gameplay ÷ first-time launches. Roblox uses a similar signal (typically 30–90 seconds).
Group join success
Group launch attempts where everyone lands in one server ÷ group launch attempts.
First meaningful action
Median and p90 time from entry to a creator-defined meaningful action. Report non-completion separately.
Week-2 return
New players in week 0 who play anywhere on the platform in week 1 ÷ new players in week 0. Uses the same calendar weeks as the North Star, so returners are counted in it.
Repeat co-play
Initial co-players who co-play again within 7 days ÷ eligible initial co-players, in a fixed cohort.

Roblox's creator retention metrics are experience-level, unlike this platform-level map. No baseline or target is claimed. More safety reports can reflect more harm or better reporting; read them with other evidence.

“Before we join” — full test design

Observation → hypothesis. In one evening, a friend answered the group’s questions before we joined. Hypothesis: groups without such a guide hesitate, start less often, or bounce early.

Current-state audit (1 Nov 2026). Public pages for DOORS, The Mimic, Pressure and Break In (Story) show genre, maturity label and descriptors, server player limit, live players, servers and store. Group size, round length, learn-as-you-go and starting together are absent or only in creator free text (DOORS: best entered “without a guide”). Re-audit on the target device before testing.

Experiment. For eligible adult groups, assign a stable group at its first eligible game-page view, before exposure. Compare the current page with the same page plus the block. Avoid cross-variant member exposure.

Outcome. Groups in one server within five minutes ÷ all randomized groups, counting non-joins and failures; one observation per group. Pair with sampled “matched expectations” feedback and check nonresponse bias.

Before launch. Set sample size, minimum worthwhile effect, duration and guardrail limits from baseline data. Monitor unwanted contact, crashes and purchase regret. Preserve all age, privacy and parental restrictions. Five minutes is a proposed window. No experiment has been run.
